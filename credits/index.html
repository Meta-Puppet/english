Music

The music played at the venue is all original music created by Suno AI, and no third party music is used. In creating this song, I referred to the texture of Kangding Ray's song and the sound implementation of NieR:Automata (localization that keeps the sound in place, and the use of deterioration filters to create an uneasy feeling). A preview of the final version is posted in Experience Implementation Record.

3D Model Assets

For some of the characters and backgrounds, POLYGON Prototype Pack (Art by Synty) is used via the Unity Asset Store.

As models for statues, the shapes of the heads of the Winged Victory of Samothrace, Laocoön, Apollo Belvedere, Michelangelo's David (head), and Put (Angel Child) are used. All of these are 3D models that are reproductions and scans of famous sculptures, and are publicly available under CC0 (equivalent to public domain) or CC-BY (free to use as long as they are indicated).
